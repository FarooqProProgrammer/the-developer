# Backend Development

Separate HTTP transport, business rules and persistence. Validate inputs at the boundary. Enforce authentication and object-level authorization server-side. Define stable errors, pagination and idempotency where needed. Use bounded timeouts and transactions. Test authorized and unauthorized requests, invalid inputs and service failure. Never expose secrets in logs.
